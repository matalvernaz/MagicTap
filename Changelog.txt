Transcendence - a second prestige layer for endgame players. Narrative Tutorial - guided 5-step intro for new players that tells the story of becoming a wizard and teaches core mechanics.
Story Milestones - 16 narrative events that trigger at key progression points (100 Mana through 1 trillion), narrating the wizard's rise to power.
Flavor Text - filled in all 22 empty upgrade descriptions with thematic text (spell schools, apparel, proficiency items).
Keyboard Shortcuts - documented in the Options panel. G=gather, letter keys for all panels.
Proficiency Indicator - Wizard Proficiency and rank now displayed on the main screen under MPS.
Effective Production Display - each building card shows real production per unit with all multipliers applied, updates live.
Buy All Affordable - button above upgrades that purchases all affordable upgrades at once.
Arcane Automation - new prestige upgrade that auto-buys cheapest building and upgrade every 3 seconds.
Progressive Disclosure - Purchased Upgrades button hidden until you have one.
Mana in tab title (only when tab is in background, to avoid screen reader noise).
Save on tab close to prevent progress loss.
